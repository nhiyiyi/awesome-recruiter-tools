## AI Recruiting Automation

Tools that use AI to automate sourcing, screening, scheduling, and candidate engagement.

- **[NinjaHire](https://ninjahire.co)** — Autonomous AI recruiter for staffing teams. Automates sourcing, outreach, screening, and scheduling. Built specifically for MSP/VMS and high-volume staffing. Free trial available.
- **[Claude MCP for Recruiting](https://github.com/ninjahire/autonomous-recruiter)** — Open-source autonomous recruiting workflow using NinjaHire's MCP server. Build your own AI recruiter in minutes.
- **[Ollama](https://ollama.ai)** — Run LLMs locally for candidate screening, resume parsing, and outreach drafting. Free and open source.
- **[LangChain](https://langchain.com)** — Framework for building AI-powered recruiting workflows. Free open-source.
- **[PromptLayer](https://promptlayer.com)** — Log, search, and evaluate AI prompts for recruiting. Free tier available.
- **[Flowmingo](https://flowmingo.ai)** — Free AI Interviewer and screening platform for recruiters. The AI drafts questions and scoring criteria from the job description, candidates answer in their own time on a phone or a laptop, and each interview returns the recording, transcript and scores with reasons. Free plan, no credit card.

- **[Wellfound (AngelList Talent)](https://wellfound.com)** — Startup talent sourcing. Free for recruiters.

## Applicant Tracking Systems

Free and open-source ATS platforms.

- **[Freshteam](https://freshworks.com/hrms/freshteam)** — Free ATS for up to 3 active jobs. Good for small teams.
- **[Zoho Recruit Free](https://zoho.com/recruit)** — Free tier for 1 active job. Solid feature set.
- **[Homerun](https://homerun.co)** — Simple ATS with free trial. Good UX.
- **[Manatal](https://manatal.com)** — AI-powered ATS. 14-day free trial.
- **[OpenCATS](https://opencats.org)** — Fully open-source ATS. Self-hosted. Free forever.
- **[CandidATS](https://candidats.net)** — Open-source ATS. Self-hosted. Free.
- **[iKrut](https://ikrut.com)** — Free ATS with unlimited jobs and users. UK-based.
- **[Recruitee](https://recruitee.com)** — Collaborative ATS. Free 18-day trial.
